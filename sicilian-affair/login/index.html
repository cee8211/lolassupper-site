A Sicilian
Affair

To die for.

It's Christmas Eve, 1948. The Moretti family and their guests are gathering at the family's lodge in the mountains north of Boston, as they do every year — part celebration, part business, the kind of night where deals get made over cocktails and old debts get called in.

This year, Zia Donatella will hand down the family's most treasured heirloom, the Moretti Diamonds, to the eldest daughter, Eva, as tradition demands. Let's hope "The Fox" — the city's most notorious jewel thief — stays far away.

A fierce storm has rolled in, cutting the lodge off from the outside world. Which is unfortunate, because Chandler Jones, Nick Johnson's valet, has just been found dead in the kitchen. It looks like a heart attack — but is it murder?
