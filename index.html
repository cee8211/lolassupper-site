01

Everyone is dealt cards — 1 on the first round, going up to a max, then back down to 1.

02

Before playing, everyone bids how many tricks they think they'll win that round.

03

Hit your exact bid: +10 + bid. Miss by any amount: −5 per trick off.

04

The dealer can't bid the number that makes total bids equal the card count. Someone always suffers.
